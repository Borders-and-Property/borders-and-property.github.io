Research team

Borders and Property brings together researchers specializing in Ottoman history, Greek history, historical geography, and computer science.

The project is organized around three complementary areas of expertise.

Ottoman history

Research on Ottoman archival sources, property relations, administrative practices, and legal processes.

Greek history and sources

Research on Greek archival materials, Greek-language newspapers, historical geography, and the administrative consequences of territorial change.

Computer science and digital methods

Development and application of computational methods for historical document processing, OCR, information extraction, data integration, and digital research tools.

The interdisciplinary collaboration is central to the project's methodology, bringing historical expertise into the design, evaluation, and application of digital methods.
